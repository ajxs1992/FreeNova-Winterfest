SERVER RULES

1. GENERAL CONDUCT

  • Respect all players. No insults, harassment or hate speech.
  • No spam in chat or voice. No advertising other servers or websites.

2. GAMEPLAY

  • No cheating of any kind: wallhack, aimbot, no-recoil, scripts.

3. FAIR PLAY

  • One account per player. Progression sharing and XP farming are punished.
  • Do not farm XP/coins with bots, friends or alternate accounts.
  • VIP perks are personal - do not trade or sell them.

4. PENALTIES

  • Gag for chat abuse, slap/slay for minor gameplay abuse.
  • Kick for repeated offenses, ban for cheating or farming.
  • Cheaters are banned permanently with no appeal.
